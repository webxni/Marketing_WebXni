# Global Editorial Policy

## Allowed pillars

- Residential: lockouts, rekeying, lock repair/replacement, deadbolts, confirmed smart/keypad locks, confirmed mailbox/sliding-door locks, and broken-key extraction from residential locks.
- Commercial: lockouts, rekeying, storefront/office lock repair and replacement, door hardware, master-key planning, property-management and apartment-building service, and confirmed electronic/access or panic/exit hardware.
- Automotive: car or vehicle lockout assistance, keys locked inside a car, and emergency vehicle entry only.

## Prohibited services

Never generate or publish key copying/duplication/cutting, car or vehicle key replacement, remote/coded/digital keys, fobs, programming/reprogramming, transponder/chip keys, ignition work, motorcycle keys, or automotive key generation. A website or provider profile cannot override this policy.

## Claims requiring evidence

Response times, prices, affordability superlatives, ratings/review counts, 24/7 availability, licensed/insured/bonded/BBB claims, years in business, superlatives, guarantees, and unapproved coverage areas require an approved, unexpired evidence record. Otherwise use: "Call to confirm current availability, service coverage, and scheduling."

## Prompt inputs

Generation may use only approved profile, monthly strategy, topic slot, services, areas, keywords, research, claims, destination, recent brand history, sibling-brand history, and global exclusions. Pending, rejected, quarantined, expired, website-derived, or stale provider records are excluded.

## Hard blocks

Prohibited service, wrong brand, wrong location, invalid phone, unsupported guarantee, unapproved claim, strategy, topic, research, or destination, and sibling-brand duplication all block saving. Any other warning requires human review before persistence.
