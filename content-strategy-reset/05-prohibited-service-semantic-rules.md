# Prohibited Service Semantic Rules

The matcher evaluates titles, descriptions, keywords, captions, scripts, blog copy, CTAs, and design prompts before prompt assembly and again before save and publish.

Block semantic combinations involving:

- `copy`, `copying`, `duplicate`, or `duplication` near `key` or `keys`
- `key cutting`
- `car`, `vehicle`, `automotive`, or `motorcycle` near `key`
- remote, coded, digital, transponder, or chip keys
- key fobs, fob creation, replacement, or programming
- key, remote, fob, transponder, or chip programming/reprogramming
- ignition repair, replacement, or service

Allowed automotive language is narrowly limited to lockout assistance, keys locked inside a vehicle, and emergency vehicle entry. "We do not replace car keys" may appear only as a corrective disclaimer; it must never become the primary keyword or service topic.

Stemming, punctuation, capitalization, and word-order variants are normalized. Literal allowlists cannot override a semantic match.
